# 角色

先用三个模板（转面、拆件、表情）给每个人出图：把模板里的［角色］换成下面这个人的描述段，［风格·角色］换成 `README.md` 里的那段。每张都是横版 3:2。

## 三个模板

### T1　转面图

上传：这个人已经有的设定图（没有就不传）；画风参考传 R1。

中文：［角色］的角色转面设定图：同一个人画三遍，从左到右依次是正面、3/4 侧身朝画面左、背面，三个人一样大，脚站在同一条水平线上，间距均匀，互不重叠。A 字站姿：双臂自然下垂、向两侧张开约 30 度，手臂和身体之间留出明显空隙，双腿分开与肩同宽；头发、围巾、衣摆不压住手臂。3/4 侧身是身体和脸一起转约 45 度（不是正侧面），两只眼睛都看得到，远侧的眼睛窄一些。［风格·角色］避免：正侧面、交叉手臂、手插口袋、手里拿东西、四肢互相重叠、透视夸张、半身截断、背景、地面阴影、文字标注、水印。

English: A character turnaround sheet of [character]: the same person drawn three times, from left to right front view, three-quarter view facing the left side of the image, back view; all three the same size, feet on the same horizontal line, evenly spaced, not overlapping. A-pose: arms hanging down and angled about 30 degrees away from the body with clear gaps between arms and torso, legs shoulder-width apart; hair, scarf and coat hems do not cover the arms. The three-quarter view turns the body and face about 45 degrees together (not a full profile), both eyes visible, the far eye narrower. [character style] Avoid: full profile, crossed arms, hands in pockets, holding objects, overlapping limbs, strong perspective, cropped body, background, ground shadow, text labels, watermark.

### T2　拆件图（要动起来的人物都出一张）

上传：图 1 这个人确认过的转面图。

中文：图 1 这个角色的二维骨骼动画拆件图，正面视角：把身体拆成下面这些部件，平铺在纯白背景上，每个部件分开、间距均匀、互不重叠，大小和图 1 一致。每个部件都画完整：被别的部件挡住的地方也补全（例如手臂后面的衣服、头发后面的脖子、刘海后面的额头）。关节处画成圆头，部件转动时接缝不露出空隙。部件：没有头发的头（带五官和两只耳朵）、刘海、两边垂在脸旁的头发、后面一整片头发、脖子、躯干（不带袖子）、衣服下摆、左上臂、右上臂、左前臂、右前臂、左手、右手（张开）、另外一对握拳的手、骨盆、左大腿、右大腿、左小腿、右小腿、左脚（鞋）、右脚（鞋）［加上这个人自己的配件：围巾、帽子、伞、包……］。［风格·角色］避免：部件互相重叠、少了部件、部件不完整、透视、文字标注、编号、水印。

English: A 2D skeletal-animation parts sheet of the character in image 1, front view: break the body into the parts below and lay them out on a pure white background, every part separate, evenly spaced, not overlapping, at the same scale as image 1. Draw every part complete, including the areas normally hidden by other parts (the coat behind the arms, the neck behind the hair, the forehead behind the bangs). Draw rounded ends at the joints so no gap shows when a part rotates. Parts: bald head with the face and both ears, bangs, the locks of hair hanging beside the face, the whole back hair as one piece, neck, torso without sleeves, coat skirt / hem, left upper arm, right upper arm, left forearm, right forearm, left hand and right hand (open), an extra pair of fists, pelvis, left thigh, right thigh, left shin, right shin, left foot (shoe), right foot (shoe) [plus this character's own accessories: scarf, hat, umbrella, bag…]. [character style] Avoid: overlapping parts, missing parts, incomplete parts, perspective, text labels, numbers, watermark.

### 路人（群像）

**R3　城里的人**（横版 3:2，用不到拆件，出一张就够）：

中文：同一部动画里的十个路人，正面和 3/4 侧身混着站成一排，脚在同一条线上、互不重叠：大人、老人、孩子都有，男女各半；人人低着头、撑着颜色各不相同的雨伞（粉、薄荷、雾蓝、杏、淡黄），穿着素淡的粉彩色衣服（不要紫色），脚上都是铁底鞋；有两个孩子脚踝上挂着金色小铃铛；有一个人手里拎着一只小哑铃。［风格·角色］避免：有人抬头、颜色太艳、互相重叠、文字、水印。

English: Ten passersby from the same animation, front and three-quarter views mixed, standing in a row with their feet on the same line and not overlapping: adults, elderly people and children, half men and half women; everyone has their head bowed and holds an umbrella of a different color (pink, mint, misty blue, apricot, pale yellow), wears soft pastel clothes (no purple), and has iron-soled shoes; two of the children have little gold bells on their ankles; one person carries a small dumbbell. [character style] Avoid: anyone looking up, overly saturated colors, overlapping, text, watermark.

**R4　她十二年里救过的人**（人链用，横版 3:2）：

中文：十个各不相同的城里人（大人、老人、少年，男女都有，衣服颜色各不相同、都是柔和的粉彩色），都光着脚，胸前用红绳挂着一小块灰白色的砖；正面站成一排，脚在同一条线上、互不重叠。［风格·角色］避免：有人穿鞋、互相重叠、文字、水印。

English: Ten different townspeople (adults, elderly people and teenagers, men and women, each in a different soft pastel color), all barefoot, each with a small off-white brick hanging on a red string at the chest; standing in a row in front view, feet on the same line, not overlapping. [character style] Avoid: anyone wearing shoes, overlapping, text, watermark.
